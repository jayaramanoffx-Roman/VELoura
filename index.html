Brand Story & Store Policies

Brand Manifesto

About VELoura — Made to Feel Like You

Architectural luxury, heavyweight fabric science, and personalized streetwear crafted for the next generation.

The VELoura Philosophy

VELoura was founded to bridge the divide between flimsy fast-fashion tees and exorbitantly expensive luxury labels. We engineer bespoke, heavyweight 220 GSM garments tailored to feel like a second skin, priced affordably for creators and student squads.

Fabric Engineering & Heavyweight Drape

Every single VELoura garment is woven with 100% Super-Combed Cotton weighing 220 GSM (Grams per Square Metre). Unlike lightweight high-street tees (140–160 GSM), our bio-washed fabric holds a structured architectural drape that flatters all frames and prevents see-through wear.

220 GSM

Bio-Washed Finish

Silicone enzyme treated to eliminate roughness, creating an ultra-soft velvety hand feel that resists fabric pilling.

Zero Shrinkage

Pre-Shrunk Terry

Heat-set and pre-shrunk before precision tailoring to guarantee consistent sizing wash after domestic wash.

HD Inks

Direct-to-Film Mastery

Japanese pigment printing produces vibrant, crack-resistant graphics with stretch recovery that outlasts standard screen prints.

Interactive Clothing Pioneers

Through our signature drops, we pioneered dynamic QR Code Memory Tees (linking to personal Spotify tracks, cloud drive memories, or symposium portals), WebAR 3D apparel, and AI-assisted typography so no two squads look generic.

Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. Fully aligned with Indian DPDP Act standards.

Zero Third-Party Data Selling

VELoura values customer privacy. We collect only the essential details required to craft, print, and courier your customized apparel. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or contact details to third-party brokers.

1. What Information We Collect

  • Contact & Shipping Data: Full Name, WhatsApp phone number, delivery address, city, and pincode.
  • Customization Content: Custom typography strings, uploaded graphic photos, or memory URLs provided within the Creator Studio for printing on your apparel.
  • Transaction Records: Payment mode selected (UPI, Cards, COD) and transaction confirmation IDs. (Note: VELoura never stores card PINs or passwords).
  • Local Storage: Cart item status, saved wishlist items, and studio canvas drafts stored locally on your device.

2. How Your Data Is Utilized

  • To accurately personalize, pack, and ship your apparel to your delivery address.
  • To transmit order status, consignment dispatch notices, and tracking alerts directly to your WhatsApp.
  • To verify customer support requests, replacements, or size inquiries.

3. Payment & Cloud Security

Digital checkout is powered by bank-grade, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant gateways using 256-bit SSL encryption. Uploaded images are stored securely during print production and never used for advertising without your written consent.

Because each garment is individually tailored and printed on-demand with your custom artwork or text, cancellations or design modifications must be submitted within twelve (12) hours of order placement before physical printing starts.

1. User-Submitted Content & Intellectual Property

By uploading artwork or text to our Creator Studio, you affirm that you own the rights or hold authorization to reproduce the design. VELoura reserves the right to decline print jobs containing hate speech, obscenity targeting minors, or unlawful intellectual property.

2. Transparent Pricing & Promo Codes

All catalogue items are listed in Indian Rupees (₹) inclusive of GST. Promotional codes (like LAUNCH10) must be applied prior to checkout.

3. Digital vs Fabric Tolerances

While our studio renderers simulate accurate placement, minor natural color variances (5–8%) can occur between illuminated phone displays and natural dyed cotton textiles under sunlight.

Common Questions

Q: How should I wash my 220 GSM VELoura tee?

Machine wash cold (under 30°C) inside out with mild detergent. Do not bleach or tumble dry. Never iron directly on printed graphics or QR badges.

Q: Will the QR code on the Memory Tee work after multiple washes?

Yes! Our high-density Japanese DTF pigments are sealed with protective silicone coatings, maintaining high scannability even after 40+ washes.

Q: Can we order custom tees for our college fest or symposium?

Yes! We offer tiered squad discounts for orders of 10+ tees with complimentary 3D mockup proofs and dedicated campus delivery.
