Brand Manifesto

About VELoura — Made to Feel Like You

Architectural luxury, heavyweight fabric science, and personalized streetwear crafted for the next generation.

The VELoura Philosophy

Streetwear in India has traditionally been trapped between two extremes: cheap fast-fashion t-shirts that shrink after one wash, or international luxury labels charging ₹4,000+. VELoura was founded to shatter this divide—offering bespoke, heavyweight 220 GSM garments engineered to feel like a second skin, priced for students and creators.

Fabric Engineering & Heavyweight Drape

Every single VELoura garment begins with 100% Super-Combed Cotton weighing a substantial 220 GSM (Grams per Square Metre). Unlike lightweight high-street tees (typically 140–160 GSM), our bio-washed fabric holds a relaxed, architectural boxy silhouette that flatters all body types and prevents see-through wear.

220 GSM

Bio-Washed Finish

Silicone enzyme washed to eliminate rough fabric fibers, creating an ultra-soft velvety hand feel that prevents pilling over time.

Zero Shrinkage

Pre-Shrunk Terry

Every roll of cotton is heat-set and pre-shrunk before cutting, ensuring your size stays exactly the same after domestic washes.

HD Inks

Direct-to-Film Mastery

Japanese pigment printing produces vibrant, crack-resistant prints with flexible stretch recovery that outlasts regular screen prints.

Interactive Clothing Pioneers

We believe apparel should be an expressive canvas. Through our interactive catalogue drops, we pioneered dynamic QR Code Memory Tees (linking to personal Spotify anthems, cloud drive galleries, or symposium websites), WebAR 3D apparel, and AI-assisted typography so that no two squads wear the exact same generic design.

On-Demand Sustainable Craft

Fast fashion generates millions of unsold garments that end up in landfills. VELoura operates on a smart, localized on-demand production model: garments are freshly printed upon customer customization, virtually eliminating unsold deadstock.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. Compliant with Indian Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act standards.

Our Core Privacy Commitment

VELoura values your trust above all else. We collect only the essential personal information needed to craft, print, and deliver your customized apparel. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third-party brokers.

1. What Information We Collect

  • Contact & Shipping Data: Full Name, WhatsApp phone number, delivery address, city, and postal pincode for dispatch.
  • Customization Content: Custom typography strings, uploaded graphics, or memory URLs provided within the Creator Studio for printing on your apparel.
  • Transaction Details: Payment method selected (UPI, Debit/Credit Card, or Cash on Delivery) and payment confirmation references. (Note: VELoura does not store bank account passwords, CVVs, or card credentials).
  • Technical Logs & Cookies: Browser session data, cart item cookies, and saved studio configurations stored locally on your device.

2. How Your Data Is Utilized

  • To accurately print, tailor, and ship your apparel to your doorstep.
  • To communicate order status, dispatch notices, and tracking links directly via WhatsApp or SMS.
  • To provide prompt customer support and resolve replacement or size requests.
  • To protect against fraudulent transactions and unauthorized chargebacks.

3. Payment & Data Security

All digital payments on VELoura are handled through encrypted, bank-grade PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways using 256-bit SSL encryption. We never see or store your raw card details.

4. Custom Artwork & Image Handling

Artwork and personal photos uploaded to the Creator Studio are temporarily stored on secure cloud servers strictly for the purpose of rasterization and physical print production. Uploaded media is never repurposed for promotional marketing without your explicit written consent.

5. Your Rights & Deletion Requests

You have the absolute right to view, modify, or permanently delete your contact history from our customer databases. Simply message our WhatsApp support desk or email privacy@veloura.in with your request.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How should I wash and care for my 220 GSM VELoura tee?

Machine wash cold (under 30°C) inside-out with gentle detergent. Avoid bleaching or tumble drying. Never iron directly on the printed motif or QR code—iron on reverse side.

Q: Will the QR code on the Memory Tee work after multiple washes?

Yes! We use high-density, sharp-contrast DTF pigments with protective silicone sealing. Our test prints maintain high scannability even after 40+ domestic washes.

Q: Can we order custom squad t-shirts for our college symposium or club?

Absolutely! We provide special bulk pricing tiers for orders of 10+ tees, complimentary digital proofs, and dedicated delivery coordination for campus events.

Q: How do I know which size to order?

Our t-shirts are tailored with a modern relaxed/boxy streetwear fit. If you prefer an oversized aesthetic, we recommend ordering one size up from your regular slim-fit size.
